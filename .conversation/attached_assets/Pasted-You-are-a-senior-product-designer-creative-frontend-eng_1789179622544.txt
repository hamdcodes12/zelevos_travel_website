============================================================
2. MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT
3D-LIKE CINEMATIC SCROLL STORYTELLING
============================================================

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART OF THE WEBSITE.

Do NOT create a normal page where images simply appear while scrolling.

The images themselves must become part of the storytelling.

The website should feel like the user is physically moving through a travel experience.


HERO IMAGE BEHAVIOR:

At the top:

A beautiful full-screen cinematic destination image occupies the entire viewport.

Hero image:

width: 100vw
height: 100vh

The image must feel immersive.

When the user begins scrolling:

DO NOT immediately hide the image.

Instead:

1. Image remains pinned.
2. Image slowly zooms.
3. Image subtly shifts position.
4. Hero text begins moving upward.
5. Hero text slowly fades.
6. AI input slightly moves and fades.
7. Image begins transforming from fullscreen into a premium rounded visual.
8. Image gradually becomes smaller.
9. Image moves toward the position of the next destination section.
10. Image transitions visually into the next storytelling section.

IMPORTANT:

There must NEVER be a large ugly empty area on the left or right while the image is shrinking.

The animation must always maintain visual balance.

The image should feel like:

FULLSCREEN IMAGE
↓
CINEMATIC ZOOM
↓
CONTROLLED RESIZE
↓
ROUNDED IMAGE
↓
DESTINATION CARD
↓
NEXT STORY SECTION


Use:

Framer Motion
useScroll
useTransform
useSpring
Motion values

Use scroll progress to control:

- scale
- width
- height
- x
- y
- border radius
- opacity
- overlay intensity
- text position
- text opacity


Do NOT use random timers for scroll animations.
